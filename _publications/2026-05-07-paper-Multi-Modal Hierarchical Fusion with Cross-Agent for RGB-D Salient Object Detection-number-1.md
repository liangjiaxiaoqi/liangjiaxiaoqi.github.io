In RGB-D salient object detection (SOD), effectively integrating complementary information from RGB and depth modalities remains a challenge. Most existing encoder–decoder frameworks fail to achieve deep interactive fusion between the two modalities in high-dimensional space. To overcome this limitation, we propose a novel multi-modal hierarchical fusion approach with cross-agent interaction, termed HMaT-D, which adopts a ternary encoder–convertor–decoder architecture. At its core, a cross-modal bilateral cross-agent Transformer convertor (C2MBA-TC) facilitates bidirectional fusion through two rounds of cross-agent token exchange, significantly surpassing conventional cross-attention mechanisms. Additionally, boundary-guided multi-level token fusion further enhances feature representation during decoding. Extensive experiments on seven benchmark datasets demonstrate that our method achieves state-of-the-art performance. The code will be available at <https://github.com/liangjiaxiaoqi/HMaT-D>.
